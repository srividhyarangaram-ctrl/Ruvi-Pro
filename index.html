Discover payments

Ruvi can detect possible payment notifications after you explicitly enable Android notification access. Notification content may include sensitive information; Ruvi stores only redacted event metadata on your device. It does not read historical notifications.

Gmail discovery

Not connected

Secure Gmail account connection is not available in this build. We will not request your password or pretend that email scanning is active.

A future release needs Google-approved OAuth integration and explicit Gmail read-only consent.

Privacy & settings

Subscription and purchase records are stored in this app's local WebView storage. Uninstalling may delete them. No cloud backup is configured.

Import replaces existing local records. Exported backups are not encrypted; store them privately.
